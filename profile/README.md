## 1. What is Advocop?

Advocop is **not** a generic legal chatbot or a standard document generator. It is a deterministic, multi-agent legal cognition system designed to assist humans in achieving **proportionate** legal outcomes. 

By ingesting government documents (traffic tickets, fines, summons) via OCR, Advocop orchestrates a swarm of specialized legal reasoning agents to expose procedural weaknesses, synthesize strategic attack vectors, and execute ready-to-file legal documents—all while preserving strict institutional trust boundaries.

> [!IMPORTANT]  
> **The Escalation Calibration Principle**  
> Advocop distinguishes between procedural correctness, strategic wisdom, and moral proportionality. The objective of the system is to calculate and execute *proportionate legal action*, optimizing for cost asymmetry, negotiation viability, and institutional balance rather than maximal aggression.

## 2. How it Works: The Multi-Agent Pipeline

The core engine is driven by a sequential orchestration pipeline. Each agent represents a distinct legal reasoning lens, ensuring that output is structurally sound and strategically adversarial.

| Phase | Agent Role | Function | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Fact Extraction** *(Vision/OCR)* | Parses raw documents (JPG/PNG/PDF) and extracts baseline data asynchronously. | Structured Fact Base |
| **02** | **Adversarial Analyst** | Conducts flaw extraction, exposing contradictions and procedural weaknesses. | Formal Error Manifest |
| **03** | **Strategic Innovator** | Synthesizes novel strategic attack angles and interpretive expansions. | 3 Strategic Pathways |
| **04** | **Logical Architect** | Assesses procedural coherence and defines the document plan dynamically. | Operational Step JSON |
| **05** | **Writer(s)** *(Execution)* | Drafts the required legal documents in parallel with exponential backoff and jitter. | Formatted Legal Text |
| **06** | **Guardian** *(Security)* | Re-injects tokenized PII and appends mandatory EU AI Act disclaimers. | Verified Final Document |

## 3. Key Moats & Capabilities

Advocop's strategic depth is its primary advantage. We avoid flat, generalized AI wrappers in favor of bounded, multi-agent orchestration.

*   **Role-Driven Cognition:** Agents like the *Adversarial Analyst*, *Strategic Innovator*, and *Logical Architect* act as rigorous legal lenses rather than general assistants. Future agents include the *Escalation Arbiter* and *Equity Analyst*.
*   **Dynamic Step Generation:** The system does not rely on static templates. The *Logical Architect* agent decides the required document count and architecture at runtime based on case severity.
*   **LexLinker Integration:** Automatically detects and hyperlinks Polish legal citations directly to source materials (e.g., lexlege.pl) to establish immediate provenance.
*   **KeyholeText UX:** Freemium users receive a blurred document preview with critical legal keywords highlighted, proving systemic value before the paywall.
*   **Golden Legal Sample:** Utilizes few-shot prompting grounded in verified Polish legal document templates to ensure formatting and tonal compliance.
*   **Provider-Agnostic Core:** While currently running on Google Gemini for superior procedural synthesis, the orchestration core abstracts the LLM, allowing for future integration of sovereign local models or regional inference providers.

## 4. Target Audience & Bifurcation

Advocop's business model and deployment topology bifurcate to serve distinct operational needs:

### 🏛️ B2C: General Public
Empowering citizens facing administrative, traffic, and civil law challenges (Polish jurisdiction primary). Provides institutional-grade defense structuring for individuals navigating procedural asymmetries against the state or large entities.

### ⚖️ B2B: Law Firms & Institutional Integration
Serving legal practices requiring bulk processing, strategic second-opinions, and draft automation. 
*   **Features:** Multi-tenant boundaries, isolated retention policies, custom jurisdiction mapping, and firm-specific formatting rules.
*   **Slack Integration:** Direct deployment into firm workspaces for asynchronous case triage and strategy generation.

---

## 5. Pricing Models

*   **B2C Freemium:** Users can upload documents and view the strategic summary and *KeyholeText* (blurred preview with highlighted keywords) for free. Full document unlock and export is gated behind a standard transaction (e.g., 39 PLN).
*   **B2B Enterprise:** Subscription-based or usage-based API billing targeting law firms, offering custom retention and direct integrations.

## 6. System Architecture: The 4 Layers

All evolution of the Advocop repository must preserve these explicit, composable, and jurisdiction-adaptive layers:

1.  **Layer 4: Human Authority** — Final review, legal sovereignty, and absolute override power. The system advises; the human acts.
2.  **Layer 3: Execution & Drafting** — Document generation, citation linking (LexLinker), and formal output compliance.
3.  **Layer 2: Legal Cognition** — Multi-perspectival reasoning orchestration (Adversarial Analyst, Strategic Innovator, Logical Architect).
4.  **Layer 1: Trust & Containment** — OCR, PII tokenization/isolation, audit trails, and jurisdiction bounds.

## 7. Limitations & Edge Cases

> [!WARNING]  
> **No Legal Representation**  
> Advocop is a legal cognition tool, not a registered attorney. It does not establish an attorney-client privilege. All outputs must be reviewed by the acting user. **Layer 4 (Human Authority)** is the final, non-negotiable step in the pipeline.

> [!CAUTION]  
> **Data Sovereignty & PII Boundaries (Current Status: Genesis)**  
> Advocop is currently upgrading its *Trust & Containment (Layer 1)*. In its current Genesis phase, users are advised to redact highly sensitive PII (beyond PESEL and postal codes) before uploading, as the OCR-first local sanitization pipeline is actively being hardened to comply fully with RODO (GDPR) standards.

> [!NOTE]  
> **EU AI Act Compliance**  
> Every document generated by Advocop explicitly states that it was structured and drafted with the assistance of Artificial Intelligence, in full compliance with the transparency mandates of the EU AI Act.
